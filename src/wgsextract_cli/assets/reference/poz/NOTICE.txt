Changes: retain only chrY intervals; omit the original mitochondrial intervals.
Coordinates are copied unchanged: BED zero-based, half-open.
hg19.bed is the original hg37 BED (GRCh37 chrY, also UCSC hg19 chrY).
Contig naming is adapted at runtime to the input's chrY/Y name.

Original reference/00README.txt credits David Vance's HG38 spreadsheet.
WGS Extract v4 manual section "To Poz BAM" describes this union as approximately
12 million bases of regions used in phylogenetic/age analyses; it is NOT all
stable Y SNP sites, an exome capture kit, or a BigY capture BED.
